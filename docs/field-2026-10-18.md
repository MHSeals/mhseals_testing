# Field test 2026-10-18: thrust coupling findings

The boat was afloat with the TUI open for stops and manual takeover; trials ran
headless on the ODROID. Raw reports and narrow bags are in
`bags/field_trials_20261018/` (`agent_*`, `claude_trim_*`, plus
`evidence-handoff.tar.gz`). No calibration was applied that day. The temporary
notes and reviews from the day are summarised here and no longer kept.

Conventions: positive yaw is counterclockwise, positive sway is left, Δwz is the
driven gyro mean minus the neutral baseline (rad/s). The per-trial wz noise floor
was about 0.04 rad/s.

## What was established

- **Mapping and reversals are correct.** Each thruster alone at ±40% turned the
  boat the way its mixer row says, and its translation sign matched.
  Canonical FL, FR, RR, RL map to outputs `[1, 3, 2, 4]`, no reversals.
- **The thrusters are balanced.** Sign-normalised yaw acceleration at 40%:

  | Thruster | forward (rad/s²) | reverse (rad/s²) |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | FL | 0.514 | 0.450 |
  | FR | 0.514 | 0.425 |
  | RR | 0.538 | 0.459 |
  | RL | 0.522 | 0.438 |

  All within about ±5% forward and ±6% reverse; reverse is about 10% weaker, as
  expected for T200s. No single thruster explains the yaw seen in translation.
- **The coupling is one-sided and grows with speed.** 80% for 5 s:

  | Direction | Raw gyro turn | Late yaw rate |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Forward | +32.0° | +0.160 |
  | Reverse | +6.1° | +0.024 (noise 0.080) |
  | Left | −60.7° | −0.334 |
  | Right | +1.2° | +0.001 (noise 0.053) |

  Forward and left turn strongly, reverse and right barely: the odd and even parts
  are about equal. Forward yaw starts slightly clockwise and turns
  counterclockwise only as speed builds; left yaw starts at once and grows.
- **Row scaling cannot fix it.** For any per-thruster model,
  yaw(surge+) + yaw(surge−) = yaw(sway+) + yaw(sway−), because both pairs run every
  thruster once each way. Measured: +0.14 against −0.32 rad/s. The cause depends on
  how the boat moves (hull drag or trim, wash between thrusters, or wind and current
  at different headings), not on how hard one thruster pushes. A leading drag at the
  bow-port corner fits every sign; the physical check of trim, list and the FL mount
  is still open. This is why `procedures/balance.py` was removed.
- **A trim column cancels it, per sign.** Holding forward 0.8 straight needed about
  −0.03 yaw (−0.0375 per unit surge), left 0.8 needed +0.08; reverse and right needed
  none. Trim authority on top of a translation is about 3.5 rad/s per unit at 0.8,
  several times what pure yaw suggests (thrust goes with e|e|), so first guesses
  overshoot. A trim proportional to the command is the right form: a column of a
  per-sign decoupling `C⁺`/`C⁻`, which the driver now supports.

## Method lessons

- Use the gyro, not odometry, for yaw: single-thruster translation (≲0.2 m/s) sits
  below the per-trial noise (about 0.4 m/s), so translation checks on isolated
  pulses rarely pass.
- Compare trials at the same heading. One forward/reverse pair that looked like a
  28° cross term had headings 49° apart; a matched pair 2° apart showed none.
- GPS at 5 Hz needs its own freshness deadline (0.5 s); a shared 0.25 s deadline
  aborted a neutral baseline.
- A mean over a transient is not a steady gain; keep the late window separately.
- Hand-edited YAML must reach the live driver snapshot and the ROS parameter
  readback, or manual driving runs a different matrix than the screen shows.

## Next boat window: thrust holds

Replay so far has only the 0.8 and 0.9 holds (yaw 0.15), which cannot separate the
linear and quadratic drag terms and leave sway+, yaw+ and yaw− with one point each.
Hold every axis-sign (surge, sway, yaw; both signs) at 0.3 and 0.7 effort, 10 s
each, in that order, with neutral between. Keep the 3 m/s and 0.7 rad/s cutoffs,
and record raw GNSS velocity, FCU odometry, the adapter output, IMU and both EKFs
so heading questions can be settled from one bag.
