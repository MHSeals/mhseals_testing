\section{Why measure the water before measuring the boat}
\label{sec:cond}

The conditions analysis estimates neutral drift, the noise of a window mean,
and stationarity from supplied samples. Readiness combines these estimates with
planned speeds to compute trust ceilings and a water verdict. These are pure
estimators; the production console has no connected sensor ingestion into the
conditions service, so its verdict remains unknown without supplied samples.
Characterization measures its own local neutral baseline for each hold.

\paragraph{Notation.} The world frame is ENU and $\psi$ is counter-clockwise
from east. The conditions code retains compass heading $\psi_c$, clockwise from
north, with $\psi=\pi/2-\psi_c$. Its world-to-body rotation is
\[
 \begin{bmatrix}v_x\\v_y\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}\sin\psi_c&\cos\psi_c\\-\cos\psi_c&\sin\psi_c\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}v_E\\v_N\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The formulas use $\psi$ unless marked otherwise. $R(\psi)$ is the body-to-world
rotation; $\Rgate$ denotes readiness.
