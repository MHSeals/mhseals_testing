\section{The calibrated velocity controller}
\label{sec:controller}

The controller clips the desired body velocity to the measured axis limits,
subtracts the configured mechanical bias and the body-frame drift, then
inverts each signed speed curve and adds PI feedback.

\paragraph{Per-direction inversion.} For each axis, the sign of the requested
water-relative speed $v$ selects the stored $(d_1,d_2)$:
\[
  w=d_1v+d_2v|v|=e|e|,\qquad F_j(v)=\sgn(w)\sqrt{|w|}.
\]
A direction without a stored curve uses the diagonal gain's linear fallback.
The observer's $V_j(e_j)$ denotes the corresponding steady speed, with the
curve selected by the effort sign.

\paragraph{The control law.} Let $\bm b_m$ be the body-fixed bias,
$\hat{\bm d}_w$ the world drift, and $\bm\epsilon=\bm v^\star-\bm v$.
With $F$ acting per axis,
\[
  \bm v^\star=\operatorname{clip}(\bm v_{\rm desired},-\bm v_{\max},\bm v_{\max}),\qquad
  \bm e_{\rm ff}=F\!\left(\bm v^\star-\bm b_m-
       \begin{bmatrix}R(\psi)^\top\hat{\bm d}_w\\0\end{bmatrix}\right),\qquad
  \bm e=\operatorname{clip}(\bm e_{\rm ff}+K_p\bm\epsilon+K_i\bm I,-1,1).
\]
With feedback disabled, only the clipped feed-forward effort is returned.

\paragraph{Drift residual.} At an accepted odometry sample, subtract the
bias and predicted steady speed, then rotate the translation residual to world:
\[
  \bm r_w=R(\psi)\left(\bm v-\bm b_m-
    [V_j(e_j)]_{j=1}^3\right)_{xy},\qquad
  \bm d_c=\hat{\bm d}_w+\frac{\Delta t}{\tau_d+\Delta t}
                 (\bm r_w-\hat{\bm d}_w).
\]
The default drift time constant is $\tau_d=5$\,s.

\paragraph{Drift-update gates.} Non-finite odometry is discarded. Learning
requires positive $\tau_d$, a positive odometry interval (capped at $\Delta t=0.2$\,s,
so a longer gap still learns as one 0.2\,s step), every target component
within $0.1$ of zero, and every effort magnitude below $0.999$. A world-frame
carried displacement leaks with a 2\,s window and accumulates measured world
velocity during eligible updates. A displacement projection greater than
$0.02$\,m along the residual latches that direction. Growth requires a residual
with positive projection on the latch, and is blocked when it points away from
a fresh obstacle within $0.5$\,m of the hull (obstacle timeout $0.5$\,s).
The candidate may shrink or turn without that growth permission:
\[
  L=\begin{cases}d_{\max},&\text{growth permitted},\\
       \min(\|\hat{\bm d}_w\|,d_{\max}),&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
  \qquad \hat{\bm d}_w^{\rm new}
   =\bm d_c\min\!\left(1,\frac{L}{\|\bm d_c\|}\right),\qquad d_{\max}=0.5\,\text{m/s},
\]
with zero retained when $\bm d_c=0$. Getting under way clears the carry and
latch; reset also clears them. Drift itself survives reset and turns.

\paragraph{Conditional integration.} Each component integrates only when its
pre-update effort is not pinned at $+1$ with positive error or $-1$ with
negative error. Otherwise its integral remains fixed:
\[
  I_j^{\rm new}=\operatorname{clip}(I_j+\epsilon_j\Delta t,-I_{\max,j},I_{\max,j}).
\]
The step interval is capped at $0.2$\,s. The default translational proportional gain is $0.8$ and
$I_{\max,j}=0.3$.
Command timeout resets the integral and effort.

\paragraph{Stale odometry.} Missing odometry or a sample older than $0.3$\,s
resets the controller and returns zero effort. The observer runs in the velocity
loop and publishes drift in the world frame.
