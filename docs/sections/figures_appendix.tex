\section{Reproducing the figures}
\label{app:figures}

Committed data support the illustrative plots. From \texttt{docs/}, run the
corresponding generator with Python; these scripts use fixed seeds and NumPy.
They illustrate the equations and are separate from the production estimators.
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
 \toprule
 script & figures\\
 \midrule
 \texttt{figures/correction/correction\_tracking.py} & tracking ghost, staleness trend and magnetic interference\\
 \texttt{figures/conditions/make\_conditions\_data.py} & windows, noise, drift, windage, stationarity and ceilings\\
 \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The simulator plant's Munk-moment diagram is drawn directly in TikZ.
