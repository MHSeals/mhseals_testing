\section{Step-response metrics}
\label{sec:step}

The controller-steps procedure asks for a velocity step of size $r$ at time $0$
and records $y(t)$. Flip the sign for negative steps so that $r>0$.
Only non-\code{None} samples in the inclusive driven window $[t_s,t_e]$
are used; times below are relative to $t_s$, with no baseline subtraction.
For $m$ retained samples the validity gate is
\[
  m<5\quad\text{or}\quad r=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\text{INCONCLUSIVE}.
\]
\begin{description}[nosep]
  \item[Rise time] $t_{90} - t_{10}$, where $t_{p}$ is the first sampled time $y\ge p\,r$
        (no interpolation); unavailable if either crossing is absent.
  \item[Overshoot] $\max\!\left(0, \frac{\max_t y(t) - r}{r}\right)$.
  \item[Settling time] the first sample after the last violation of the band
        $|y-r|\le\max(0.05\,r,\ 0.02)$. It is zero if every sample is inside, and
        unavailable if the last sample is outside.
  \item[Steady-state error] $r$ minus the mean of samples $\lfloor0.8m\rfloor,\ldots,m-1$;
        positive means the boat falls short.
  \item[IAE] the integral of absolute error over the available samples, by the trapezoid rule:
        $\sum_k \tfrac12\left(|r-y_k| + |r-y_{k+1}|\right)(t_{k+1}-t_k)$.
\end{description}

A valid window is SETTLED exactly when a settling time exists:
\[
 \text{verdict}=\begin{cases}\text{SETTLED},&t_{\rm settle}\ne\text{None},\\
 \text{NOT SETTLED},&t_{\rm settle}=\text{None}.\end{cases}
\]

\paragraph{Checking against theory.} For a first-order response
$y = r(1-e^{-t/\tau})$: no overshoot, rise time $\tau\ln 9$, and infinite-horizon IAE
$r\tau$. For an underdamped second-order system with damping ratio $\zeta$, the
overshoot is $e^{-\pi\zeta/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}$ and the 5\% settling time is about
$3/(\zeta\omega_n)$. The unit tests generate these curves and require the
metrics to match.
